= Business Flows & Domain Rules
:description: Business Analyst and Product guide to FF Logistic: end-to-end freight forwarding flows, domain lifecycles, GS1 and EN 16258 / ISO 14083 rules, and tenant role permissions.

This guide is written for **Business Analysts, Product Owners, and domain reviewers**. Every business rule, status transition, and permission below is implemented once inside the shared domain cores (`shared/domain/*`) and `shared/reporting`—so it behaves identically whether the cluster runs the modular monolith or microservices.

Each business rule carries a stable ID (e.g. `BR-TMS-04`) so requirements, test cases, and change requests stay traceable.

== End-to-end freight forwarding lifecycle

A shipment crosses four business areas—**Standards**, **Warehousing (WMS)**, **Transportation (TMS)**, and **Routing (GEO)**—and feeds **Analytics, BI & Monthly Reporting**:

[mermaid]
....
flowchart LR
  ASN["1. ASN announced\n(EXPECTED)"] --> REC["2. Goods received\n& palletized (SSCC)"]
  REC --> SHP["3. Shipment planned\n(multimodal legs + CO2e)"]
  SHP -->|Flow 1: road distance| GEO["Routing distance\n(OSRM / haversine)"]
  SHP --> RTE["4. Delivery route\nplanned (Timefold)"]
  RTE --> STOP["5. Route stop completed\n+ e-POD uploaded"]
  STOP -->|Flow 3: RouteStopCompleted| DEL["6. Shipment DELIVERED\n+ e-CMR"]
  DEL -->|Flow 2: ShipmentDelivered| ARR["7. Warehouse inbound\narrival opened (OPEN)"]
  DEL -->|Kafka| ANA["8. Analytics RLS rollup\nSuperset + S13 PDF & Mailpit"]
....

.Cross-area business flows
[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Flow | Trigger | Business effect

| *Flow 1* | Booking a road leg by origin/destination coordinates | Transportation queries Routing (`DistanceLookup`) for road distance; falls back to straight-line estimate (haversine × 1.24) if Routing is unavailable and records the source (`ROUTING` vs `ESTIMATE`) on the leg.
| *Flow 2* | A shipment moves to `DELIVERED` with a destination warehouse GLN | Warehousing idempotently opens an `OPEN` inbound arrival at the receiving dock (`ShipmentDelivered`). No manual re-entry is required.
| *Flow 3* | A driver completes a route stop linked to a shipment code | Routing publishes `RouteStopCompleted`; Transportation transitions that shipment to `DELIVERED`, which automatically triggers Flow 2 and Analytics ingestion.
| *Reporting (S13)* | Planner generates a monthly PDF report (`yyyy-MM`) | Reads `tenant_delivery_monthly` under PostgreSQL RLS, renders the PDF once per tenant/month into RustFS, and notifies configured tenant channels (Email / Webhook) via Kafka `report-ready`.
|===

== Domain state machines

=== Warehousing: ASN & Inbound Arrival

* **ASN (`AsnStatus`)**: `EXPECTED` → `RECEIVING` → `RECEIVED`
  ** `EXPECTED`: announced by the supplier; nothing received yet.
  ** `RECEIVING`: at least one pallet has been built and placed in a bin; remaining lines are still open.
  ** `RECEIVED`: every line has received its announced quantity; additional receiving against the ASN is refused.
* **Inbound Arrival (`ArrivalStatus`)**: `OPEN` — opened automatically by Flow 2 when a shipment carrying a warehouse GLN reaches `DELIVERED`.

=== Transportation: Shipment

* **Shipment (`ShipmentStatus`)**: `PLANNED` → `PICKED_UP` → `DEPARTED` → `DELIVERED`
  ** Strictly forward-only: intermediate steps may be skipped (for example, a carrier reports departure directly without a separate pickup), never repeated and never moved backwards.
  ** `DELIVERED` is terminal and records the consignee acknowledgement (`receivedBy`).

=== Routing: Route Stop

* **Stop (`StopStatus`)**: `PLANNED` → `COMPLETED`
  ** Completing a stop records delivery handoff (`receivedBy`) and—when linked to a shipment—triggers Flow 3.

== Business rules catalogue

[cols="1,4,2",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Rule | When violated

| `BR-SEC-01` | Every business action requires a valid JWT verified locally against Identity's JWKS. | Rejected (`401`)
| `BR-SEC-02` | Every endpoint enforces role-based access via `@PreAuthorize`. | Rejected (`403`)
| `BR-SEC-03` | A user only reads or mutates their own tenant's data; cross-tenant IDs appear absent. | `404` or empty list
| `BR-SEC-04` | Tenant IDs strictly match `[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]{1,63}` (`TenantIds`). | Rejected (`401`)
| `BR-WMS-01` | Warehouse GLN has 13 digits and a valid GS1 Mod-10 check digit. | Rejected (`400`)
| `BR-WMS-02` | Product GTIN has 8, 12, 13, or 14 digits with a valid GS1 check digit. | Rejected (`400`)
| `BR-WMS-03` | Every received pallet receives a unique, sequence-backed 18-digit GS1 SSCC. | Rejected (`400`)
| `BR-WMS-04` | Receiving all announced ASN quantities closes the ASN as `RECEIVED`; further receipts are refused. | Rejected (`409` / `400`)
| `BR-TMS-01` | Container/trailer equipment codes follow ISO 6346 with a valid check digit. | Rejected (`400`)
| `BR-TMS-02` | Shipment pallet SSCC has 18 digits and a valid GS1 check digit. | Rejected (`400`)
| `BR-TMS-03` | Shipment numbers come from PostgreSQL sequences and are never reused. | —
| `BR-TMS-04` | CO₂e per leg = `distanceKm × tonnes × modeFactor`; savings compare against all-road (`ROAD`). | —
| `BR-TMS-05` | Shipments progress strictly forward (`PLANNED → PICKED_UP → DEPARTED → DELIVERED`); steps may be skipped, never reversed. | Rejected (`409`)
| `BR-GEO-01` | Total stop demand cannot exceed `vehicleCapacityKg`; routes cap at `99` stops (`BR-GEO-02`). | Rejected (`400`)
| `BR-GEO-03` | Routes with > 8 stops or any time window (`readyMinutes`/`dueMinutes`) are optimized by Timefold; shorter routes use nearest-neighbour. | Rejected (`400` if infeasible)
| `BR-DOC-01` | Legal documents (e-CMR, e-POD) are **write-once**: presigned PUT signs `If-None-Match: *`. | `409` (app) / `412` or `403` (RustFS)
| `BR-REP-01` | Analytics tables enforce PostgreSQL `FORCE RLS`; `set_config()` is revoked from `PUBLIC`. | `0` rows or `42501`
| `BR-REP-02` | Monthly PDF reports build once per `(tenant, yyyy-MM)` from `tenant_delivery_monthly` (`201`); repeats return the stored report (`200`) without re-notifying. | —
| `BR-REP-03` | Per-channel delivery (`email`, `webhook`) is idempotent via `sent_delivery(report_id, channel)`; failed sends retry then move to `.dlt`. | Dead-letter topic
|===

== Industry standards & emissions calculation

All domain standards live once in `ff-domain-standards`:

* **GS1 identifiers (`Gs1Standards`)**:
  ** **SSCC** (18-digit Serial Shipping Container Code) with Mod-10 check digit generated automatically for every received pallet.
  ** **GLN** (13-digit Global Location Number) with check digit identifying warehouses and consignee docks (used to route Flow 2 arrivals).
* **ISO 6346 container codes (`Iso6346ContainerCode`)**: validates owner prefix, equipment category (`U`/`J`/`Z`), serial digits, and check digit (e.g. `CSQU3054383`).
* **Greenhouse gas footprint (`EmissionsCalculator`)**:
  ** Implements **EN 16258 / ISO 14083 (GLEC Framework)**: sums `distanceKm × grossWeightTonnes × factor` across all legs, compares against a 100% road baseline (`ROAD`), and calculates `savedCo2eKg`, `reductionPercent`, and `treeYearsSaved` (`21.77 kg CO₂e / tree-year`).
  ** Both Superset dashboards (S8) and monthly PDF reports (S13) read the pre-computed `tenant_delivery_monthly` rollup—no duplicate carbon formula exists downstream.

[cols="2,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Transport mode | g CO₂e / tonne-km | Description

| `ROAD` | 62.0 | Road, heavy goods vehicle (baseline)
| `RAIL` | 22.0 | Rail, intermodal block train
| `INLAND_WATERWAY` | 28.0 | Inland waterway barge
| `SEA_RORO` | 31.0 | Sea, Ro-Ro or short-sea vessel
| `AIR` | 602.0 | Air cargo
|===

== Multi-tenancy & role permission matrix

Every authenticated request carries a `tenant_id` claim verified locally against Identity's JWKS (xref:security.adoc[Security & Multi-Tenancy]). Operations are guarded by method-level `@PreAuthorize` rules:

[cols="2,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Role | Primary areas | Allowed operations

| `WAREHOUSE_OPERATOR` | Warehousing | Warehouses, bins, ASNs, pallet receiving, inbound arrivals (`/api/v1/wms/**`)
| `LOGISTICS_PLANNER` | Transportation, BI & Reporting | Shipments, multimodal legs, e-CMR links, road distance lookup, Superset guest-token exchange (`POST /api/v1/guest-tokens`), monthly PDF reports & channels (`/reporting/api/v1/reports/**`)
| `DISPATCHER` | Routing | Territories, Timefold route plans, stop completion, e-POD links, road distance lookup (`/api/v1/geo/**`)
| `REPORT_VIEWER` | BI & Reporting | Monthly PDF reports and tenant analytics access
| `TENANT_ADMIN` | All tenant areas | Full access to WMS, TMS, GEO, Reporting, plus tenant user and integration-client management (`/api/v1/users`, `/api/v1/integration-clients`)
|===
