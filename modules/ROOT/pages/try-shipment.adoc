= Try it: a shipment to a warehouse, step by step
:description: The S4 walkthrough as numbered steps: a shipment delivered to a warehouse opens an inbound arrival there, in-process on the modulith, over Kafka on the services.

Flow 2 of xref:architecture.adoc#flow-2[the architecture]: a shipment is booked to the warehouse of xref:try-warehousing.adoc[Try it: a warehouse] (`IST-HDK`, GLN `8690001000011`), travels, and is delivered; the delivery opens an *inbound arrival* at that warehouse's dock. First on the modulith, then on the services, against the same edge.

Run one block at a time and compare its answer with the ✅ line. The ✅ lines say what to expect; where a value depends on the run (ids, times), it says so.

Everything runs in one WSL terminal; the later steps use the variables the earlier ones set.

[[before]]
== Before: the release and the schemas

The S4 images are rolled out (`argocd app sync logistics-app`, xref:walkthrough.adoc#s4[walkthrough]).

=== 1. The new tables, and the modulith's outbox

[source,bash]
----
PSQL="kubectl -n ff-logistics exec deploy/postgres -- psql -U fflogistics -d ff_logistics -c"
$PSQL "select 'transportation' area, version, description from transportation_flyway_history
       union all select 'warehousing', version, description from warehousing_flyway_history where version::int >= 3
       union all select 'modulith outbox', version, description from modulith.outbox_flyway_history
       order by 1, 2"
----

✅ Transportation at version 2 (its tables), warehousing at 4 (the inbound arrivals), and a new schema `modulith` with its own outbox history:

----
      area       | version |      description
-----------------+---------+------------------------
 modulith outbox | 1       | event publication
 transportation  | 0       | << Flyway Baseline >>
 transportation  | 1       | transportation begins
 transportation  | 2       | transportation tables
 warehousing     | 3       | warehousing pallets
 warehousing     | 4       | warehousing inbound arrivals
----

=== 2. A token, and two shortcuts

[source,bash]
----
E=localhost:8081
TOKEN=$(curl -s -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"username":"nexus.planner","password":"fflogistics2026"}' localhost:8085/sessions | jq -r .accessToken)
H=(-H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json')
curl -s "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/wms/warehouses | jq '.[] | {code, gln}'
----

✅ The warehouse from S3 is still there: `{"code": "IST-HDK", "gln": "8690001000011"}`. `nexus.planner` is both a logistics planner and a warehouse operator of `ff-eu-freight`.

[[modulith]]
== On the modulith

=== 3. Book a shipment to the warehouse

From Cologne to the Istanbul hub: by road to the rail terminal, by train to Trieste, by Ro-Ro ferry to Istanbul, the last 60 km by road. `destinationGln` names the warehouse.

[source,bash]
----
SHP=$(curl -s "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/tms/shipments -d '{"consignorName":"Rhein Parts GmbH",
  "consigneeName":"Istanbul Hadimkoy Hub","destinationGln":"8690001000011",
  "equipmentCode":"CSQU3054383","sscc":"106141411234567897","incoterm":"DAP",
  "origin":"Cologne Eifeltor","destination":"Istanbul Hadimkoy","grossWeightKg":18000,
  "legs":[
    {"mode":"ROAD","origin":"Cologne Eifeltor","destination":"Cologne Eifeltor Terminal","distanceKm":15,"carrierReference":"DE-K-FF-210"},
    {"mode":"RAIL","origin":"Cologne Eifeltor Terminal","destination":"Trieste","distanceKm":1050,"carrierReference":"TXL-41877"},
    {"mode":"SEA_RORO","origin":"Trieste","destination":"Istanbul Pendik","distanceKm":1450,"carrierReference":"DFDS-PT-2611"},
    {"mode":"ROAD","origin":"Istanbul Pendik","destination":"Istanbul Hadimkoy","distanceKm":60,"carrierReference":"TR-34-FF-0417"}]}')
echo "$SHP" | jq '{shipmentNumber, ecmrNumber, status, destinationGln, footprint}'
ID=$(echo "$SHP" | jq -r .id)
----

✅ The first shipment of a fresh sequence, planned, with its footprint:

----
{
  "shipmentNumber": "SHP-10001",
  "ecmrNumber": "ECMR-10001",
  "status": "PLANNED",
  "destinationGln": "8690001000011",
  "footprint": {
    "totalDistanceKm": 2575.0,
    "co2eKg": 1308.6,
    "roadOnlyCo2eKg": 2873.7,
    "savedCo2eKg": 1565.1,
    "reductionPercent": 54.46
  }
}
----

The footprint (EN 16258 / ISO 14083 factors per transport mode): 18 t over 2,575 km by rail and sea emits 1.3 t CO2e, less than half of what the same trip by road alone would.

=== 4. It travels

[source,bash]
----
curl -s -X POST "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/tms/shipments/$ID/pickup | jq -r .status
curl -s -X POST "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/tms/shipments/$ID/departure | jq -r .status
----

✅ `PICKED_UP`, then `DEPARTED`.

=== 5. Delivered at the warehouse

[source,bash]
----
curl -s "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/tms/shipments/$ID/delivery -d '{"receivedBy":"Dock 2, A. Yilmaz"}' | jq '{status, deliveredAt}'
curl -s "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/wms/arrivals | jq '.[0] | {shipmentNumber, sscc, grossWeightKg, status}'
----

✅ The shipment `DELIVERED`, and an arrival open at the dock, for the same shipment:

----
{
  "shipmentNumber": "SHP-10001",
  "sscc": "106141411234567897",
  "grossWeightKg": 18000.0,
  "status": "OPEN"
}
----

Nobody called warehousing: transportation published `ShipmentDelivered`, the `flows` module heard it after the commit and opened the arrival. An empty list means the second call was quicker than the listener; ask again.

=== 6. What the outbox recorded

[source,bash]
----
$PSQL "select listener_id, status, completion_attempts, completion_date is not null as completed
       from modulith.event_publication order by publication_date"
----

✅ One row, for the flow's listener (`…flows.DeliveriesToWarehouses.on(…ShipmentDelivered)`), `COMPLETED` after one attempt. The pickup and the departure published `ShipmentProgressed` too, but nothing in the modulith listens to it, so the registry stored nothing for them.

=== 8. The same, through the services

The token has expired by now: step 2's `TOKEN=` and `H=` again. Then a second shipment, the same plan with another order of the day:

[source,bash]
----
SHP2=$(echo "$SHP" | jq '{consignorName, consigneeName, destinationGln, equipmentCode, sscc, incoterm, origin,
  destination, grossWeightKg, legs: [.legs[] | {mode, origin, destination, distanceKm, carrierReference}]}' \
  | curl -s "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/tms/shipments -d @-)
ID2=$(echo "$SHP2" | jq -r .id); echo "$SHP2" | jq -r .shipmentNumber
curl -s -X POST "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/tms/shipments/$ID2/departure > /dev/null
curl -s "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/tms/shipments/$ID2/delivery -d '{"receivedBy":"Dock 2, A. Yilmaz"}' | jq -r .status
sleep 2; curl -s "${H[@]}" $E/api/v1/wms/arrivals | jq '[.[] | {shipmentNumber, status}]'
----

✅ `SHP-10002`, `DELIVERED`, and now two arrivals: the one the modulith opened, and the one the warehousing service opened from Kafka:

----
[
  {"shipmentNumber": "SHP-10002", "status": "OPEN"},
  {"shipmentNumber": "SHP-10001", "status": "OPEN"}
]
----

=== 9. What travelled over Kafka

[source,bash]
----
kubectl -n ff-logistics exec kafka-0 -- /opt/kafka/bin/kafka-console-consumer.sh \
  --bootstrap-server localhost:9092 --topic ff.logistics.tms.shipment-delivered \
  --from-beginning --max-messages 1 --property print.key=true --timeout-ms 10000
----

✅ One message: the key is the shipment's id (`$ID2`), the value transportation's `ShipmentDelivered` as JSON, with `destinationGln`, `sscc` and `grossWeightKg`. Only one: the modulith's delivery in step 5 never left its JVM.

[source,bash]
----
kubectl -n ff-logistics exec kafka-0 -- /opt/kafka/bin/kafka-consumer-groups.sh \
  --bootstrap-server localhost:9092 --describe --group warehousing
----

✅ The group `warehousing` on the topic, `LAG` 0 on every partition: every message has been read.

=== 10. The outbox on this side

[source,bash]
----
$PSQL "select listener_id, status, completion_attempts from transportation.event_publication order by publication_date"
----

✅ Rows for the Kafka externalization (here the listener is the sending itself, not a business listener), `COMPLETED`: the departure's `ShipmentProgressed` and the delivery's `ShipmentDelivered`, each sent once.

[[database]]
== What the database says

=== 11. Two arrivals, one from each side

[source,bash]
----
$PSQL "select a.shipment_number, a.status, a.created_at, w.code
       from wms_inbound_arrivals a join wms_warehouses w on w.id = a.warehouse_id order by a.created_at"
----

✅ `SHP-10001` and `SHP-10002` at `IST-HDK`. The rows do not say which side opened them: the same core opened both, called once by a listener in the same JVM, once by a Kafka consumer in another process.
